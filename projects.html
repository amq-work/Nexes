SaaS / On-Demand UI/UX Design Development 2024

Nestly - For Labors

A Blueprint for Intelligent On-Demand Household Services Dispatch and Subscription-Based Home Care.

The Problem

Traditional local home maintenance suffers from lack of scheduling speed, opaque price bidding, high consumer search friction, and inconsistent quality in repair crews.

The Solution

We designed and built a full-stack digital hub that manages, schedules, and dispatches vetted home care experts using real-time geolocation matching, upfront estimate calculators, and multi-tier subscription models.

Key Features

  • Real-time geolocation matching and dispatching
  • Dynamic upfront labor and material cost calculator
  • Multi-tier subscription model (Standard, Prime, Estate)
  • Live dispatch map tracker and progress stepper
  • Seamless technician self-registration and profile matching
  • Automated verification and background safety checks
